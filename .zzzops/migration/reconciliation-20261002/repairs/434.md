## Outcome
Provide safe phase ownership and brief storage serialization without duplicate work.

## Scope and acceptance
Persist renewable leases for every phase on goals; advisory next steps acquire nothing. Explicit starts bind coordinator identity then worker identity. Serialize storage writes across machines, wait/re-read on contention and reject stale owners. Independent batches give per-item results and idempotent retries; reject dependent batches. One local heartbeat per coordinator uses local liveness probes to renew shared leases. Keep executable commands local; stop on release/replacement, reconcile completed workers, and require recovery after uncertain liveness rather than automatically reassigning expired work. Retry mechanics are internal, not policy knobs.

## Baseline and falsifiable verification
Current ZzzOps uses separate commands and prose-driven execution; #429 did not ensure the real agent followed delegation. Use concurrent-client and process fixtures to prove single ownership, no lost updates, batch retry behavior, probe handoff, heartbeat cleanup, completed-worker reconciliation and no duplicate dispatch after unknown/expired liveness.
