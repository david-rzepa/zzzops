Test-cost evidence (G1043): optional boolean verification.parallel_commands, measured integer-millisecond checks timing and independently qualified whole-run/repository baseline comparisons, and shipped measured-cost review contexts. Presence of parallel_commands, even false, requires test_cost; checks.run nodes then declare both verification.commands and verification policy dependencies. The default template requires test_cost but keeps parallel_commands absent until a repository explicitly approves concurrent command safety.
