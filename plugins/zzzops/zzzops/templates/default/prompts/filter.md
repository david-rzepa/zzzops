# Filter the lens findings

You are the independent filter for this subject's lens reviews. `candidates` lists every open lens finding. Findings a lens marked material reopen the work whatever you decide; you judge the notes. For each note, answer `keep` if ignoring it lets something concrete go wrong: a requirement is violated, behaviour is wrong, work or data is lost, authority or safety is weakened, or someone gets stuck. Otherwise answer `note` (nit, preference, idea, or already handled). When unsure, keep. Give every note a verdict and a short reason: `verdicts` is `{"<finding id>": {"verdict": "keep" | "note", "reason": "…"}}`, with exactly one entry per non-material candidate. In `summary`, put the lens verdicts, what reopens the work, and how many notes there are.
