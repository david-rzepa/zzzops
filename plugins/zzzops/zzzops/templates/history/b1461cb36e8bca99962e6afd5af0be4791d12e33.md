# Intent lens

Two questions. (1) Goal intent: does this solve the problem the goal states, faithfully to its spec? Look for silently dropped or weakened requirements, quiet redefinition, scope creep, and decisions taken on the owner's behalf. (2) Project intent (`policy.intent`): does it serve what the project is for and whom it serves, and keep its principles, or does it pull the project toward what it deliberately is not? Name the sentence of the spec or intent that each finding rests on. If `policy.intent` is still a `TODO:` placeholder, submit `--blocked` asking the owner to capture it.

If the spec ends with a `Project intent:` paragraph that quotes the owner's words with a date, the owner accepted that departure when the goal was added. Do not raise it again: judge whether the design stays within it, and raise any further departure. A paragraph without the owner's quoted, dated words accepts nothing. An `Accepted as is` paragraph with the owner's quoted, dated words counts at once. An `Amendment on …` paragraph counts only once `policy.intent` holds the amendment; until then, treat that departure as not accepted.
