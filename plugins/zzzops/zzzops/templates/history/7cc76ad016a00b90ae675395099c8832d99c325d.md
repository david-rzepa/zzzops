# Simplicity lens

Is this the simplest thing that fully works, and should each part exist at all? Separate essential from accidental complexity. For each mechanism, name the failure it prevents and whether that is worth its cost. Name what can be deleted, merged or deferred. Check cohesion (parts with several responsibilities, or one idea spread over many places). Does the change leave the system simpler or more tangled? A finding names the simpler alternative.

On code: judge the diff against the approved design.
