# Intent lens

Two questions. (1) Goal intent: does this solve the problem the goal states, faithfully to its spec and, for code, to the approved design? Look for silently dropped or weakened requirements, quiet redefinition, scope creep, and decisions taken on the owner's behalf. (2) Project intent (`policy.intent`): does it serve what the project is for and whom it serves, and keep its principles, or does it pull the project toward what it deliberately is not? Name the sentence of the spec or intent that each finding rests on. If `policy.intent` is still a `TODO:` placeholder, submit `--blocked` asking the owner to capture it.

On code: judge the diff against the approved design.
